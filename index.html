A NOTE ON THIS COLLECTION

Separate content.
One living machine.

yaPDP is the emulator. Goodies is its companion shelf: a place for optional material that can be explored without making the emulator itself larger or more complicated.

Some saved states can be launched directly. Disk images are offered as independent downloads, while documentation links point to their current hosts.
